## Control-code scoreboard fields

Across every emitted instruction: how often each 3-bit scoreboard field holds an index (anything but 7 = unused). Bits are LSB-first over the two 64-bit words. Instructions whose *result* arrives late (loads, MUFU, conversions, S2R) set bits 110-112; instructions that read their *source registers* late (stores, atomics, LDGSTS) set bits 113-115. So 110-112 is the write/result barrier and 113-115 the read barrier, as in Jia et al. (the kuterdinel pages label them the other way round). Consumers wait via the mask in bits 116-121.
